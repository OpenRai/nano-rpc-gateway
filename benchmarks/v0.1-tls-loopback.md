# v0.1 TLS transport benchmark

Status: **LOCAL HARNESS ONLY**

Captured 2026-08-29 on an arm64 MacBook Air (8 logical CPUs, 8 GiB RAM). The
release gateway handled 20 sequential `account_info` requests over a local
self-signed TLS listener, with a fixed V28.2-shaped native-RPC stub. The two
runs use the same gateway and stub; they measure gateway plus TLS transport
overhead, not Nano-node or production capacity.

Commands:

```text
PROTOCOL=http1.1 INSECURE_TLS=1 REQUESTS=20 \
  OUTPUT_FILE=benchmarks/v0.1-tls-h1.raw \
  GATEWAY_URL=https://127.0.0.1:18444 ./scripts/benchmark.sh

PROTOCOL=http2 INSECURE_TLS=1 REQUESTS=20 \
  OUTPUT_FILE=benchmarks/v0.1-tls-h2.raw \
  GATEWAY_URL=https://127.0.0.1:18444 ./scripts/benchmark.sh
```

Observed request wall time (seconds):

| Protocol | Samples | p50 | p95 | Min | Max | Mean | Stddev |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| HTTP/1.1 | 20 | 0.002753 | 0.004909 | 0.002513 | 0.004969 | 0.003004 | 0.000667 |
| HTTP/2 | 20 | 0.002795 | 0.003272 | 0.002621 | 0.003280 | 0.002886 | 0.000208 |

Raw samples are retained in [v0.1-tls-h1.raw](v0.1-tls-h1.raw) and
[v0.1-tls-h2.raw](v0.1-tls-h2.raw). This does not measure SSE fan-out, idle
RSS, startup time, native-node load, or container performance.
