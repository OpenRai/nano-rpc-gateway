# v0.1 process resource probe

Status: **LOCAL GATEWAY-ONLY**

Captured 2026-08-29 on an arm64 MacBook Air (8 logical CPUs, 8 GiB RAM). Ten
sequential starts of the release binary were measured until `/health` replied;
the process was then sampled with `ps` before shutdown. The native HTTP and
WebSocket upstreams were intentionally unreachable, so these values exclude
Nano-node memory and startup costs.

Command:

```text
for _ in $(seq 1 10); do ./scripts/resource-benchmark.sh; done
```

Results:

| Measure | Samples | Min | Max | Mean | Stddev |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Startup to `/health` (ms) | 10 | 13 | 27 | 16.0 | 4.2 |
| Idle RSS (KiB) | 10 | 10016 | 10096 | 10049.6 | 29.0 |

Raw samples are retained in [v0.1-resource.raw](v0.1-resource.raw). This is a
process-only probe, not a container, native-node, or SSE fan-out measurement.
