# v0.1 loopback benchmark

Status: **LOCAL HARNESS ONLY**

Captured 2026-08-29 on an arm64 MacBook Air (8 logical CPUs, 8 GiB RAM,
Darwin 25.5.0). The release gateway binary handled 20 sequential
`account_info` requests over HTTP/1.1. The native endpoint was a local stub
that returned a fixed V28.2-shaped result; this is a gateway-overhead smoke
measurement, not a Nano-node or production-capacity claim.

Command:

```text
REQUESTS=20 OUTPUT_FILE=benchmarks/v0.1-loopback.raw ./scripts/benchmark.sh
```

Observed request wall time (seconds):

| Samples | p50 | p95 | Min | Max | Mean | Stddev |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 0.000736 | 0.000821 | 0.000662 | 0.003141 | 0.000862 | 0.000524 |

The raw samples are retained in `v0.1-loopback.raw`. HTTP/2, SSE fan-out,
RSS, native-node load, and cross-machine/container measurements remain
untested.
